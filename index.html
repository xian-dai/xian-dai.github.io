Xian DAI 戴娴

I am currently a postdoctoral research fellow in Université Côte d'Azur, France ( funded by the ERC grant project Ansur of François LABOURIE and Jérémy TOULISSE ) .

Previously, I was a postdoc member in Heidelberg University, Germany, from January 2021 to July 2022 and a postdoc member in Ruhr-Universität Bochum, Germany, from August 2022 to August 2024.

I got my Ph.D. degree from Rice university, United state, in May 2020, under the supervision of Michael WOLF .

My research interests are in geometry and dynamics of (higher) Teichmuller spaces and negatively curved manifolds.

On AI and mathematics

I do not use AI in my research and do not plan to use it in the future. I enjoy thinking, exploring and discussing with human collaborators, and I do not want to offload any of these to other kinds of beings.
